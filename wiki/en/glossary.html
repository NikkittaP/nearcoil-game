Glossary

The words the game uses, as they appear on screen in English and in Russian.

TermIn RussianMeaning
CLOSE CALLSСБЛИЖЕНИЯPassing your head through a cell right next to (not diagonal to) your own older body, an obstacle or a live structure. The core move: it charges the meter.
GRAZEВПРИТИРКУThe word a close call shows. Each body cell pays once.
SKIMСКОЛЬЖЕНИЕRiding along a line of body: close calls on consecutive steps. Charge less each but keep the meter from draining.
SQUEEZEЗАЖИМDriving 2–5 cells with something on both sides of the head.
THREADСКВОЗЬDriving through a gap that splits the board in two: there is no short way round.
NEEDLEИГЛАA corridor of 6 cells or more with something on both sides.
CHAIN NСЕРИЯ NManeuvers less than 30 steps apart: each next one pays more, up to ×2.
CHARGEЗАРЯДThe segmented meter above the board. Risky moves fill it; it drains when you play safe.
MULTIPLIER ×NМНОЖИТЕЛЬ ×N×1…×8, set by how full the charge meter is. Multiplies every point and adds speed.
NODEУЗЕЛThe red target (the food). Eating it scores 10 × multiplier and grows the body.
HUNGERГОЛОДLong without eating, close calls against your own body pay less and less. Eat to restore them.
INSULATORSИЗОЛЯТОРЫFixed blocks on Campaign boards. Brush them for charge, never hit them. A brushed block goes dark for a while.
PYLON / DOORСТОЛБ / ДВЕРЬA gate is two rows of lethal pylons; the door is the passage between them. Its depth is its length.
TERMINAL / POSTКЛЕММА / СТОЙКАRelay targets. A touched terminal turns into a live post: lethal, but good for close calls.
GATEВОРОТАCircuit Event: drive straight through the door between the pylons.
RELAYРЕЛЕCircuit Event: touch numbered terminals in order; touched ones stay live.
HOT ZONEГОРЯЧАЯ ЗОНАCircuit Event: a square that banks charge and points while your head is inside; leave to cash in.
LIVE WIREПРОВОД ПОД ТОКОМCircuit Event: a moving lethal bar; pass right next to it three times.
CHOOSEВЫБЕРИModule ports appear after a won event: drive into one to take its module.
PHASEФАЗАModule: your next crash into a conductor hops over it instead.
SURGEФОРСАЖModule: about 8 s faster, with more charge and more points.
CUTСРЕЗModule: the next node you eat cuts off the oldest part of your body.
CASHВЫПЛАТАLeaving a hot zone pays out what it banked.
BURNEDСГОРЕЛОStill inside when a hot zone overloads: the bank is lost.
FUSESПРЕДОХРАНИТЕЛИCampaign lives. Failing a level you have not cleared yet burns one. They come back over time.
PASSЗАЧЁТFirst medal of a level; clearing it opens the next level.
GREATОТЛИЧНОSecond medal of a level.
PERFECTИДЕАЛTop medal; reaching it ends the level at once.
RANKEDНА РЕКОРДEndless mode with one world leaderboard.
DAILYИСПЫТАНИЕ ДНЯOne board a day, the same for everyone; three counted tries.
LEAGUEЛИГАWeekly groups of about 30 players; the top moves up a tier, the bottom moves down.
