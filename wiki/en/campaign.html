All 70 levels of the Campaign, with their time limits, the three targets and what each board changes compared with Ranked. Names and hints are the ones you see in the game.

How to read the Board column: only what differs from the standard rules is listed. Events lists the Circuit Events the level uses (with the allowed variants in brackets); ports lists the modules that can be offered. Percentages scale the speed, every charge gain or the drain. A level ends when its time is up, when you crash, or at once when you reach PERFECT — see Campaign rules.
