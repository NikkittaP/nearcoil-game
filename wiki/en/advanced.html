This page contains detailed mechanics intended for players who want to optimize leaderboard runs. You do not need to know these values to enjoy or play NEARCOIL effectively — the rest of the guide explains everything that matters in plain words.

Speed

The base step time depends only on how many nodes you have eaten:

Nodes eatenStepCells per second

The multiplier adds +5% at ×2 up to +30% at ×8 (see the table), and SURGE another +14%. The step never gets shorter than 84 ms.

step = base step × 100 / (100 + multiplier bonus % + SURGE bonus %), at least 84 ms

"About N s" on this page assumes a typical early step of 150 ms.

After the 10th node every node adds 2 cells.

  • A node is never closer than 4 cells (in steps) to the head.
  • Cells right next to your body and at middle distance (up to 11 cells) are weighted up.

Swipe controls fire once the finger has moved about 16 dp.

  • Charge from — charge needed for that multiplier.
  • Close-call charge kept — the share of charge from close calls, skims and tight steps you get at that level (maneuvers and events are not reduced; inside a hot zone close calls always charge in full).

Hold and drain

  • After a gain the meter holds for 16 steps (about 2.4 s). A gain from close calls buys a hold shortened by the same close-call share; a maneuver or an event buys the full hold.
  • After dropping a level there is a 10-step breather before the next level starts draining.
  • Hunger: close play against your own body pays in full for 200 steps (about 30 s) after the last node, then fades linearly to zero over the next 200 steps (about 30 s).

Close-call and maneuver detection

  • The 5 body cells right behind the head never count as a close call.
  • A board cell that just produced a close call can't produce another for 60 steps.
  • A close call within 3 steps of the previous one is a SKIM.
  • An insulator re-arms 40 steps after it paid.
  • Gap length: SQUEEZE 2–5 tight steps, NEEDLE 6+, THREAD 1–4 with no way round shorter than 10 cells.
  • A maneuver continues the chain if it comes less than 30 steps after the previous one.

Circuit Event schedule

  • The first event starts after 95 steps (about 14.2 s) of free play.
  • Between events: 95–140 steps, 6 fewer for every event you complete, never below 70. An event that ended while you were at ×5 or more adds 18 calm steps.
  • New structures appear at least 4 cells from your head.
  • The kinds come from a shuffled bag with no repeats in a row; LIVE WIRE joins after the first 2 events.
  • Intensity = events completed so far, +1 while you are at ×5 or more. It unlocks the harder variants below; the weight is the variant's share in the draw.
