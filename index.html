NEARCOIL

A Snake arcade game with close-call scoring. Move next to your own body to build the multiplier, then survive the run. Includes a campaign, an endless Classic mode, ranked leaderboards, daily challenges, and replays.

Google Play (closed testing) Game guide Privacy policy Contact the developer

Currently in closed testing — the Play link works once you've joined as a tester. The Android release is coming soon; iOS follows later.

See it in motion

Every turn you take next to your own body charges the multiplier: GRAZE, SQUEEZE, THREAD, NEEDLE. Eat to bank the points, and don't get trapped in your own coil.

The video plays from YouTube once you press play; nothing is loaded from YouTube before that. More videos on the channel.

Features

Campaign

70 hand-made levels in 7 chapters: gates, circuits, moving boards, power modules and a final meltdown. Up to three medals each.

Classic

Endless snake with no limits, no ads and no leaderboard: just play, as long as you like, even offline. Classic keeps its own best score.

Ranked

World, country and weekly leaderboards, with weekly leagues as the player base grows. Tap a name to watch how that run was played.

Daily challenge

One board a day, the same for everyone. Three attempts count, then practice until tomorrow.

Replays & clips

Watch any run at 1×, 2× or 4×, jump to the best moment, and share a result card or a short vertical video.

Controls

Flick the way you want to go, or tap the left / right half to turn. Smooth on 90 and 120 Hz screens.

Offline & cloud save

Campaign and Classic play anywhere; scores and progress catch up when you are back online. Save your progress with Google or Apple to keep it on a new phone.

Ads & purchases

No banners, no forced ads: the only ad is one you choose to watch for an extra Ranked run. Daily is always free, everyone has the same daily Ranked limit, and nothing you can buy changes the score.

Game guide

Close calls, the multiplier, SQUEEZE / THREAD / NEEDLE, Circuit Events, modules and all 70 Campaign levels, with the exact numbers: read the game guide · справочник на русском.

Themes

Signature and LCD are free. Six premium themes each bring their own look and sound — cosmetic only, they never change the game.

Privacy in short

No registration. Online boards use an anonymous account; if you want your progress to survive a new phone, you can save it with Google or Apple in Settings (only then is your e-mail kept). Gameplay statistics can be switched off in Settings, and DELETE ONLINE DATA erases everything on the server at once. The optional rewarded ads come from Google AdMob, whose SDK may collect identifiers such as the advertising ID; in the EEA, UK and Switzerland it asks for your consent first.
