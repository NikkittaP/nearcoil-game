Ports ({{ t('CHOOSE') }})

{{ img('ports', 'Two module ports after a won event: choose by steering.') }}
  • Ports open after a won event (on Campaign levels that allow modules). One or two appear {{ Circuit.PORT_REACH[0] }}–{{ Circuit.PORT_REACH[1] }} cells from your head, preferably on opposite sides of your heading, so it is a real fork.
  • You have {{ steps(Circuit.PORT_TIME) }} to steer into one. Driving into a port takes its module; the other port disappears.
  • What can be offered: {{ t('PHASE') }} unless you already carry one; {{ t('SURGE') }} always; {{ t('CUT') }} only when your body is at least {{ Circuit.CUT_MIN_LEN }} cells long and you don't carry one.
  • You carry at most one PHASE and one CUT at a time. The module you hold is shown on the right of the event bar.

{{ t('PHASE') }}

{{ img('phase', 'Lesson 4-01: carrying a PHASE, the head hops over a row of insulators.') }}

While you carry a PHASE, your next crash into a conductor does not end the run: the head hops over that one cell and lands on the far side.

  • Works on any conductor: your body, pylons, live posts, bridge bars, wires and insulators. It hops exactly one cell, so the cell beyond must be free.
  • Drive into the board edge instead and the PHASE takes you through it: you come back in on the opposite side, same row or column, for the same payout. The charge is spent on the edge, so if your body or a conductor sits where you come in, the run ends.
  • A conductor with no free cell behind it still kills. One PHASE, one use: after it, walls kill again.
  • The hop pays +{{ Circuit.PHASE_PTS }} points (× multiplier) and +{{ Circuit.PHASE_FLOW }} charge — use it on purpose, not only as a spare life.

{{ t('CUT') }}

{{ img('cut', 'Lesson 4-05: the next node cuts off the oldest part of the body.') }}

The next node you eat after taking CUT severs the oldest part of your body.

  • It removes {{ pct(Circuit.CUT_PCT) }} of the body, the oldest part (the tail end), but never leaves fewer than {{ GameSim.START_LEN }} cells.
  • Every severed cell discharges into the meter: +{{ Circuit.CUT_FLOW_PER_CELL }} charge per cell.
  • It frees room on a crowded board; the price is less body to skim along.
