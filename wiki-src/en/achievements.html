How they work

  • {{ len(achievements.list) }} achievements in {{ len(achievements.categories) }} categories: {{ t('ACH_CAT_CORE') }}, {{ t('ACH_CAT_NEARCOIL') }}, {{ t('ACH_CAT_DAILY') }}, {{ t('ACH_CAT_CAMPAIGN') }} and {{ t('ACH_CAT_SPECIAL') }}. Open them with {{ t('NAV_ACHIEVEMENTS') }} on the main menu; swipe left or right to change the tab.
  • They are checked when a run ends: a new one appears as a card over the results (three or more as one stack). Nothing pops up during a run.
  • Score, survival and multiplier achievements count in {{ t('RANKED') }} and the {{ t('DAILY') }} only: Campaign levels pay differently and some start charged.
  • Badges have one to three rims (the tier, ● in the tables below); tier three has a gold rim. Two achievements are hidden and show as ??? until you earn them.
  • Tap a badge to see it up close: what it asks, the date you earned it, or how far along you are.
  • Progress comes from what the game already counts, so an update never takes an achievement away and earlier play counts.

{{ img('profile', 'Your profile: records, every achievement and the play stats.') }}
  • Your own page opens with {{ t('NAV_PROFILE') }} on the main menu. Another player's opens from any leaderboard: tap their row, then {{ t('PF_TITLE') }}.
  • On it: their best badge as the emblem, name and country, since when they play and their league; the records ({{ t('RANKED') }} with the place in the world, this week, the {{ t('DAILY') }} and the days played, the {{ t('CAMPAIGN') }} with medals); every achievement with the day it was earned; and the play stats.
  • The records come from the leaderboards. Achievements, Campaign medals and stats are what the player's game reports: they are shown as they are and never rank anyone.
  • The share button makes the page an image to send. {{ t('PF_REPORT') }} on someone's page reports a name.
  • Players taken off the boards have no public page.
