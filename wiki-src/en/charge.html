The meter

Charge is a number from 0 to {{ GameSim.FLOW_MAX }}. The meter shows it as segments, one per multiplier step; filling a segment raises the multiplier by one.

Multiplier levels

{{ flow_table() }}
  • Charge from — charge needed for that multiplier. ×2 takes about three ordinary close calls; ×5 and above need maneuvers and events.
  • Speed bonus — how much faster the snake moves at that multiplier.
  • Close-call charge kept — the share of charge from close calls, skims and tight steps you actually get at that level (maneuvers and events are not reduced).
  • Drain — charge lost per step once the hold time is over: less while your head is within 2 cells of a conductor (setting up the next move), more in open space.

During SURGE every gain is ×{{ Circuit.SURGE_GAIN_PCT / 100 }}. Some Campaign levels scale all gains (e.g. "charge 130%").

Holding and draining

  • After a gain the meter holds for {{ steps(GameSim.FLOW_GRACE) }}. A gain from close calls buys a shorter hold at higher levels (the same share as the charge kept); a maneuver or an event buys the full hold.
  • Inside a gap (a tight step) the meter never drains.
  • After the hold the meter drains every step by the amount in the table above.
  • Levels are lost one at a time: after dropping a level there is a {{ GameSim.FLOW_DROP_GRACE }}-step breather before the next one starts draining.
  • Inside a {{ t('HOLD ZONE') }} the meter does not drain while your head is in the square.

Keeping a high multiplier means chaining: a close call or maneuver every couple of seconds. Skimming along a lane keeps the meter steady even when each skim adds little.

Machine stages

{{ img('slam', 'Reaching ×3: the giant ×N slams into free space for half a second.') }}

The look of the board follows the multiplier in stages, so you can feel your level without reading the meter:

  • ×1 — calm, clean board.
  • ×2 — the stage engages: a relay double-blink.
  • ×3–×4 — charged: heartbeat pulses run down the body, surges race round the screen edges.
  • ×5 and up — overload: white-hot core, sparks and arcs between neighbouring segments, flashes on all edges.

Every upgrade slams a giant ×N into the part of the board away from your head. Dropping a stage plays a power collapse: the edges go out and a dark wave runs from head to tail. All of this is cosmetic and the same in every theme; Settings can reduce shake and flashes.

Hunger

{{ img('hud', 'A healthy run: nodes eaten regularly, close calls pay in full.') }}

Close play against your own body fades if you go long without eating. For {{ steps(GameSim.HUNGER_GRACE) }} after the last node (or the start) it pays in full; then over the next {{ steps(GameSim.HUNGER_FADE) }} its value falls steadily to zero. The next node brings it back in full at once.

Hunger reduces the points of close calls and of SQUEEZE / THREAD / NEEDLE against your body. It does not touch:

  • charge (the multiplier still climbs);
  • nodes, every Circuit Event payout and PHASE;
  • close calls against pylons, posts, wires and insulators;
  • anything inside a hot zone, and levels without nodes.

The game shows it: the node starts to ripple its brackets (faster and brighter the hungrier you are), body arcs dim, and the first close call at half value or less says {{ t('HINT_HUNGRY') }}. Normal play eats far more often than this and never notices; it exists so that circling along your own body forever does not pay.
