This page contains detailed mechanics intended for players who want to optimize leaderboard runs. You do not need to know these values to enjoy or play NEARCOIL effectively — the rest of the guide explains everything that matters in plain words.

Speed

The base step time depends only on how many nodes you have eaten:

{{ speed_table() }}

The multiplier adds +{{ GameSim.FLOW_SPEED_PCT[1] }}% at ×2 up to +{{ GameSim.FLOW_SPEED_PCT[-1] }}% at ×{{ len(GameSim.FLOW_LEVELS) }} (see the table), and SURGE another +{{ Circuit.SURGE_SPEED_PCT }}%. The step never gets shorter than {{ GameSim.MIN_INTERVAL }} ms.

step = base step × 100 / (100 + multiplier bonus % + SURGE bonus %), at least {{ GameSim.MIN_INTERVAL }} ms

"About N s" on this page assumes a typical early step of 150 ms.

Growth and node placement

{{ growth_table() }}

After the {{ len(GameSim.GROWTH) }}th node every node adds {{ GameSim.GROW_LATE }} cells.

  • A node is never closer than 4 cells (in steps) to the head.
  • Cells right next to your body and at middle distance (up to 11 cells) are weighted up.

Swipe controls fire once the finger has moved about {{ int(Main.FLICK_DP) }} dp.

Multiplier thresholds

Charge is a number from 0 to {{ GameSim.FLOW_MAX }}.

{{ flow_table() }}
  • Charge from — charge needed for that multiplier.
  • Close-call charge kept — the share of charge from close calls, skims and tight steps you get at that level (maneuvers and events are not reduced; inside a hot zone close calls always charge in full).
  • Drain — charge lost per step once the hold is over: the lower value while your head is within 2 cells of a conductor, the higher one in open space.

Hold and drain

  • After a gain the meter holds for {{ steps(GameSim.FLOW_GRACE) }}. A gain from close calls buys a hold shortened by the same close-call share; a maneuver or an event buys the full hold.
  • After dropping a level there is a {{ GameSim.FLOW_DROP_GRACE }}-step breather before the next level starts draining.
  • Hunger: close play against your own body pays in full for {{ steps(GameSim.HUNGER_GRACE) }} after the last node, then fades linearly to zero over the next {{ steps(GameSim.HUNGER_FADE) }}.

Close-call and maneuver detection

  • The {{ GameSim.MIN_AGE }} body cells right behind the head never count as a close call.
  • A board cell that just produced a close call can't produce another for {{ GameSim.GRAZE_COOLDOWN }} steps.
  • A close call within {{ GameSim.SKIM_GAP + 1 }} steps of the previous one is a {{ t('SKIM') }}.
  • An insulator re-arms {{ GameSim.WALL_REARM }} steps after it paid.
  • Gap length: {{ t('SQUEEZE') }} {{ GameSim.SQUEEZE_MIN }}–{{ GameSim.NEEDLE_MIN - 1 }} tight steps, {{ t('NEEDLE') }} {{ GameSim.NEEDLE_MIN }}+, {{ t('THREAD') }} 1–{{ GameSim.THREAD_MAX }} with no way round shorter than {{ GameSim.DETOUR_MIN }} cells.
  • A maneuver continues the chain if it comes less than {{ GameSim.CHAIN_WINDOW }} steps after the previous one.

Circuit Event schedule

  • The first event starts after {{ steps(Circuit.FIRST_EVENT) }} of free play.
  • Between events: {{ Circuit.GAP_MIN }}–{{ Circuit.GAP_MAX }} steps, {{ Circuit.GAP_SHRINK }} fewer for every event you complete, never below {{ Circuit.GAP_FLOOR }}. An event that ended while you were at ×5 or more adds {{ Circuit.RECOVERY_HOT }} calm steps.
  • New structures appear at least {{ Circuit.SAFE_DIST }} cells from your head.
  • The kinds come from a shuffled bag with no repeats in a row; {{ t('LIVE WIRE') }} joins after the first {{ Circuit.WIRE_AFTER }} events.
  • Intensity = events completed so far, +1 while you are at ×5 or more. It unlocks the harder variants below; the weight is the variant's share in the draw.
